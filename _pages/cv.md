Associate professor in machine learning at the Department of Physics and Technology, UiT The Arctic University of Norway. Group leader of the [UiT Machine Learning Group](https://machine-learning.uit.no), head of the Applied Physics and Mathematics study program, and principal investigator in the [SFF Integreat](https://www.integreat.no/) Centre of Excellence and the [SFI Visual Intelligence](https://www.visual-intelligence.no/) centre.

* ORCID: [0000-0003-1395-7154](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1395-7154)
* 27 peer-reviewed publications with over 1000 citations (h-index 15). See [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.no/citations?user=gUd35ngAAAAJ&hl=no) for the complete list.
